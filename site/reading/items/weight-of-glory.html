The Weight of Glory — C. S. Lewis

Created January 3, 2016 · Updated November 25, 2026 · 651 words

This book is a collection of sermons, talks, and standalone pieces from C.S. Lewis dating 1939 to 1956. The title chapter was preached by Lewis at Evensong in the University Church in Oxford on June 8, 1941, during the Second World War. The title comes from 2 Corinthians 4:17.

Despite the diversity of time and audience, there seems to be a running theme. Lewis wants us to see that ordinary life (study, war, ambition, belonging, forgiveness, etc.) looks different in the light of eternity.

This is the central point in the title essay, where Lewis argues that our deepest longings point beyond anything in this world. The Christian promise of “glory” is the only answer.
