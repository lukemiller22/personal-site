Notes from the Tilt-a-Whirl — N. D. Wilson

Created February 13, 2016 · Updated November 12, 2026 · 390 words

N. D. Wilson’s nonfiction books are wonderful examples of Christian belief and practice transposed into different genres. Every time I read them, they go back on the reread list.

This book, Notes from the Tilt-a-Whirl, is a way of seeing the world. It’s a statement of faith. A mosaic of propositions and stories about the world God has made.

His other nonfiction book, Death by Living, is a way of living in the world. It’s a practical theology. An invitation to receive God’s gifts and embrace them with courageous faith and fierce joy.

Both are wonderful expressions of beauty and truth. We need more theology written in genres like this.
