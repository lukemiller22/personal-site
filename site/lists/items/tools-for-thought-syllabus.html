Tools for Thought Syllabus

Created November 19, 2026 · Updated November 19, 2026 · 147 words

  • Tools for Thought by Howard Rheingold
  • “As We May Think” by Vannevar Bush
  • “Augmenting Human Intellect: A Conceptual Framework” by Douglas Engelbart
  • Computer Lib / Dream Machines by Ted Nelson
  • Mindstorms by Seymour Papert
  • Orality and Literacy by Walter J. Ong
  • “Visualization and Cognition: Thinking with Eyes and Hands” by Bruno Latour
  • Understanding Comics by Scott McCloud
  • “The Extended Mind” by Andy Clark and David Chalmers
  • “Inventing on Principle” (talk) by Bret Victor
  • Thinking in Systems by Donella H. Meadows
  • A Pattern Language by Christopher Alexander
  • Technopoly by Neil Postman
  • Preface to Plato by Eric A. Havelock
  • The Educated Mind by Kieran Egan
  • Plans and Situated Actions by Lucy Suchman

Notes

Recommendations pulled from this thread.

Syllabus covers mostly the history of computational tools for thought (Bush, Engelbart, Nelson, etc.), but it also touches on the older foundational question about how media shape thinking (Ong, Havelock, Egan, etc.).
